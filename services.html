Service 01 / Hiring & Executive Recruitment

Hire with intelligence, not instinct.

Enlyst Smart Recruiting excels in executive searches, specialising in C-level and Staff+ engineering roles — particularly in the fast-paced tech and AI sector. Our team handles demanding HR assignments with precision. Committed to bias-free recruitment, we leverage agentic sourcing and data science to produce mutually beneficial outcomes for employers and candidates.

  • Role architecture and calibrated job description design before any sourcing begins
  • Active multi-channel passive pipeline discovery — not passive job-board posting
  • Competency-based behavioural screening with pre-set rubrics locked before interviews
  • Anonymised candidate dossiers — presenting a structured case, not a forwarded CV
  • Interview coordination, structured feedback loops, offer closing & counter-offer insulation
  • EOBI and Social Security compliance on every employment contract
  • 30-day and 90-day post-placement accountability check-ins
